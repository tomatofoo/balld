# Balld

![Demo](/videos/demo.mp4)

This is a top-down ball physics puzzle game where you redirect balls that come 
from the top to a win zone near the bottom. I used pygame-ce for the graphics 
and a custom Verlet integration physics engine for the physics.

## How to Play

Drag and drop the pieces from your inventory into the field. Press "Try" to 
begin a trial. Press "Reset" to reset your piece placement. If you beat the 
minimum score required for the level, you win.
